## Summary

This guideline describes how water damage claims are handled at Nordlicht Insurance.
For water damage claims above €10,000, an assessor must inspect the damage before payout.
All other water damage claims are decided by the claims handler without an assessor
and take around 18 days.

## Purpose

This guideline applies to all water damage claims reported on or after 1 July 2023, in the
products "Residential Building Comfort" and "Home Contents Plus".

## The rule

For every water damage claim with an estimated amount above €10,000, an assessor must inspect
the damage on site before any payout is approved.

- The claims handler orders the assessor as soon as the estimated amount is known.
- The waiting time for an assessor appointment is about two weeks.
- The claim is only decided once the report is available.

Water damage claims up to €10,000 are handled without an assessor: the claims handler decides
based on photos, invoices and the repair quote.

## Processing time

| Water damage claim | Typical processing time |
|---|---|
| Up to €10,000, no assessor | around 18 days |
| Above €10,000, with assessor | around 5 to 6 weeks |

Only a few water damage claims are above €10,000, so the assessor step has little effect on
the average processing time.

## Exceptions

- **Emergency measures:** drying and securing the property may start immediately and do not
  need to wait for the assessor.
- **Advance payment:** for claims that take longer, an advance payment can be made before
  the claim is closed.
