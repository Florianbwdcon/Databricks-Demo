## Summary

This page describes in simple words what happens between the moment a customer reports
a claim and the moment the claim is closed: report, check cover, estimate, inspect,
decide, pay out, close. It also says when an assessor is required and how long each
damage type typically takes.

## The steps

| Step | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Report | The customer reports the damage by phone, app or letter. The claim gets a claim number and the reported date is recorded. | Customer, service team |
| 2. Check cover | The claims handler checks whether the policy is active and whether it covers this kind of damage. | Claims handler |
| 3. Estimate | The claims handler collects photos, invoices and repair quotes and sets the estimated amount. | Claims handler |
| 4. Inspect | If an assessor is required, the assessor inspects the damage on site and writes a report. Otherwise this step is skipped. | Assessor |
| 5. Decide | The claims handler decides whether and how much is paid. If the damage is not covered, the claim is rejected. | Claims handler |
| 6. Pay out | The amount is paid to the customer or directly to the repair company. | Payments team |
| 7. Close | The claim is closed and the closed date is recorded. | Claims handler |

Processing time is measured from step 1 (reported date) to step 7 (closed date).

## When is an assessor required?

| Damage type | Assessor required |
|---|---|
| Water damage | above €3,000 estimated amount (since 1 March 2026, before that above €10,000) |
| Storm/Hail | above €10,000 estimated amount |
| Fire | always |
| Glass breakage | never |

The details for water damage are in the page "Claims Handling Guideline Water Damage".

## How long does it take?

| Damage type | Typical processing time |
|---|---|
| Glass breakage | around 8 days |
| Water damage without assessor | around 18 days |
| Storm/Hail | around 20 days |
| Water damage with assessor | around 5 to 6 weeks |
| Fire | around 45 days |

The inspection in step 4 is the main reason why some claims take longer: the waiting time
for an assessor appointment alone is about two weeks.

## Advance payment

If a claim takes longer, the customer can receive an advance payment a few days after the
report. The rest is paid when the claim is closed.

## Rejected claims

A claim is rejected if the damage is not covered by the policy. Rejected claims get no payout
and do not count towards the processing time.
